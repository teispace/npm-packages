# Photograph credits

These are real photographs, not renders, and they are not this package's work.
Each is used under its own licence, which is **not** the MIT licence the code
carries. They live here only as test fixtures and are excluded from the
published package — `files` in `package.json` ships `dist`, `README.md`,
`LICENSE` and `CHANGELOG.md`, and nothing from `__tests__`.

Only **CC BY** and public-domain images are accepted here. CC BY-SA is
deliberately avoided: its share-alike term is a licence question nobody should
have to think about when cloning a QR library.
